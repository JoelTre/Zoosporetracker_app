Process video

Compare each frame with a background built from randomly sampled frames throughout the video. Processing happens on your device.

The background uses the median pixel value from sampled frames. More samples and higher resolutions use more memory. The output contains video only.
Overlays
Clusters with centres closer than the cluster merge threshold are combined, including chains of nearby clusters. Size and shape limits apply to the combined cluster. Trails use plasma colours from dark purple (slow) to yellow (at or above the colour maximum). Marker boxes are red when the principal-axis width-to-length ratio is below 0.5, and teal at 0.5 or above. Labels mark possible merges in red and nearby tracks in orange; dashed grey boxes mark undetected tracks held at their last position. Measurements for undetected frames are unavailable.
